Contact and support

Get help with Hisar.

For help with the Hisar iPhone, Android or Mac app, browser extension, command-line tool or server, email {{ support_email_link }}. You do not need to sign in or have a GitHub account to contact us.

Tell us what went wrong

Include the app version, your device and operating system version, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the exact error message. Tell us whether you use Hisar's hosted service or a self-hosted server. If relevant, include the server address, but never a sign-in or invitation link.

Remove personal information and secrets from screenshots and logs before sending them. Never send your master password, Secret Key, Emergency Kit, vault exports, passwords, one-time codes, private keys or share links. Support does not need them.

Email is not Hisar's end-to-end encrypted vault channel. See our privacy policy for how support messages and other data are handled.

Sign-in, sync and account help

Check that you are using the server address and account email from your Emergency Kit. If sync fails, check your internet connection and whether your server is available. Do not delete the app or its local data while troubleshooting: your device may hold changes that have not synced.

We cannot recover your master password or decrypt your vault for you. Keep your Emergency Kit safe; signing in with it still requires your master password.

For an account on a server run by your employer or someone else, contact that server's operator about invitations, availability and account administration. You can still email us for help with the Hisar software.

Privacy and account deletion

To delete your account in the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes the account on your server, not just the app from your device. Export anything you need to keep first, and review the consequences for shared vaults in our privacy policy.

For privacy questions, requests about your personal data, or help deleting a hosted-service account, email {{ support_email_link }}. For an account on someone else's server, send the account request to its operator. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account, but will not ask for your master password or Secret Key.

Security reports and public issues

Report a suspected vulnerability privately to {{ site.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}, with the subject “Security report”. Do not publish vulnerabilities, account details or secrets in a public issue.

For non-sensitive bug reports and feature requests, you can also use GitHub issues. For setup and usage instructions, see the Hisar documentation.

Who you are contacting

tosunlab, in the Netherlands, develops Hisar and runs this website. {{ support_email_link }} is our contact for product support, privacy questions and security reports.

Service status

Hisar doesn't have a public status dashboard yet. This page tells you how to check the service yourself, and what keeps working while it's down.

Check the sync service

The server answers its health check with ok while it's up and serving requests. Open {{ health_url }}, or from a terminal:

{% call code_block("Health check command") -%} $ curl {{ health_url }} ok {%- endcall %}

Anything else, or no answer, means the service is down or unreachable from where you are.

When our server is down, your apps keep working offline: unlock, SSH, git signing, hisar run and autofill use what's already on your devices. Edits, sharing and iPhone approvals wait for the server.

Email
Invitations and sharing invites		Emails are delayed.

Incidents

During the invite-only beta, we write to affected users directly about incidents and planned maintenance. If the health check says ok but something isn't working for you, email {{ site.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.

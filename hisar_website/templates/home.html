Agents use your secrets. They never see them.

Hisar is an end-to-end encrypted password, SSH key and secrets manager for developers. Every time your terminal, your editor or a coding agent asks for a key, Hisar shows you exactly which app is asking, and you approve it on your Mac or your iPhone.

SSH agent that names the app

"iTerm2 wants to sign a commit": with the app's icon, the process chain and its code signature. Approve with Touch ID or from your iPhone.

Secrets in your commands, not your files

hisar run -- npm test injects secrets from references like hisar://Work/Stripe/api key and masks them in the output.

Built for coding agents

hisar mcp lets Claude Code and Cursor run commands with your secrets. They only see vaults you allow, and every use asks you first.

End-to-end encrypted

Your vaults are encrypted on your devices with your master password and Secret Key. The server stores data it can't read.

Privacy · Effective 28 October 2026

Your vault is encrypted. Some service data is not.

This page explains privacy for the Hisar apps, this website and support. Hisar encrypts vault contents on your devices before syncing them. Running the service still requires account information and other readable metadata.

Vault contentsEnd-to-end encrypted, including titles, websites and vault names.
Service dataAccount, device, sharing and security information is readable by your server's operator.
Your choicesChoose your server, control optional features, export your items or delete your account.

Who is responsible

tosunlab, in the Netherlands, develops Hisar and runs this website. The hosted service at eu.tryhisar.com is operated by Alper Berber, the controller of its personal data. Its full privacy policy explains the service's records, purposes, retention and your rights. Contact {{ support_email_link }} for product, website or hosted-service privacy questions.

If you use a self-hosted server, its operator is responsible for account administration, hosting, logs, backups and its privacy notice. The hosted-service providers and retention periods below do not describe every self-hosted installation.

Encrypted contents and readable information

Passwords, usernames, notes, one-time-code secrets, passkeys, SSH private keys, item titles, websites and vault names are encrypted before upload. Normal sign-in does not send your master password or Secret Key to the server. We cannot reset your master password or recover your encrypted vault for you.

The server can read your account name and email, device names and platforms, session records, who has access to each vault, organization and team names and membership, invitations, item counts and sizes, change times, and security events with IP addresses. Share-link records include their creator, encrypted contents, expiry and view limits. Notification registrations are described below.

People you share with can read the contents you give them access to and may keep copies. Organization members can see their organization's membership information. Server administrators can manage accounts and see service metadata; their administrator role does not itself give them vault decryption keys.

Hosting, email and international transfers

Our hosted service uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting, its database and backups in Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1), with backup copies in Stockholm, Sweden (eu-north-1).

Cloudflare proxies eu.tryhisar.com and push.tryhisar.com and provides DDoS protection. It terminates TLS at its edge and can see IP addresses, request metadata, session tokens and other unencrypted service data in transit. Vault contents remain end-to-end encrypted in those requests. Cloudflare Email Service delivers transactional emails and processes recipients' addresses and message contents. These emails are not end-to-end encrypted and do not contain vault contents. Apple delivers iPhone notifications.

AWS, Cloudflare and Apple are US-based providers. EU database storage does not mean all processing stays in the EU: Cloudflare may handle requests at data centers outside the EU, and our proxy is not restricted to EU locations. For covered transfers, AWS and Cloudflare use the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, with Standard Contractual Clauses as fallback; UK safeguards include the UK extension or UK Addendum where applicable. Apple describes Standard Contractual Clauses for European and UK transfers in its privacy policy. See the hosted-service policy for provider links and how to request information about safeguards.

Notifications

When you enable notifications, your server stores the phone's relay handle (iPhone) or Web Push address and subscription keys (Android), along with the notification types you choose. Turning off all notification types in Hisar removes the phone's notification registration from the server when the change reaches it. This does not itself revoke Apple's push token or unregister your Android UnifiedPush distributor.

For iPhone notifications, Hisar's relay processes the Apple push token, notification type and time; Apple also sees the type and timing for that token. Additional details, such as the sender of a sharing invitation, are encrypted to the phone. Push messages do not include vault contents or the contents of approval requests. Approval details are retrieved separately through your server, end-to-end encrypted. The relay and its network proxy also process connection information, including IP addresses.

On Android, your server sends encrypted Web Push messages to the UnifiedPush service you choose. That service can see arrival times, message sizes and the notification type in the HTTP Topic header, but cannot decrypt the message body. Hisar does not use Firebase for these notifications.

On your devices and other connections

  • The apps keep an encrypted local copy of synced vaults for offline use. Face ID, Touch ID and Android biometrics are handled by your operating system; Hisar does not receive your face or fingerprint data.
  • For iPhone AutoFill suggestions, Hisar gives iOS login and passkey identifiers, including websites and usernames. Android and browser AutoFill process the current website or app and form information to match and fill credentials. Saving a login or linking it to an Android app stores the information you choose in your encrypted vault.
  • Website icons are enabled by default. Your device requests them from the relevant websites and any public hosts they use for icons or redirects, which see its IP address. Turn this off in Settings → Appearance → Show website icons. Android may also fetch a website's /.well-known/assetlinks.json to check whether an app may use its credentials.
  • The browser extension exchanges matching and filling information with the Hisar app on your computer. It keeps pairing information and settings in the browser's local extension storage.
  • The Mac app checks your server for updates. Software downloads and platform app stores involve their respective providers; GitHub downloads are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Hisar does not include advertising or usage-analytics trackers, sell personal data, or use your vault contents for profiling.

Retention and account deletion

The hosted service keeps account and encrypted vault data while the account or vault exists. Earlier item versions are normally retained for 90 days and security logs for one year, with scheduled cleanup. Access logs are kept for 14 days. Encrypted nightly database snapshots and point-in-time recovery backups are copied to a separate locked AWS account in Stockholm. All backup copies expire within 30 days; deleted data may remain in them until then.

Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account, or with hisar account delete. Vaults for which you are the only owner are deleted, including access for people you shared them with. Vaults with another owner remain. Your devices, sessions and access are removed; retained shared records can still contain your account ID. Security logs and backups follow their retention schedules. Deletion cannot erase copies other people already downloaded. Uninstalling an app does not delete your server account.

This website and support messages

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Standard access logs include IP addresses and request information and are kept for 14 days to operate and protect the site.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot and handle security or privacy issues, and retain correspondence only as needed for those purposes or legal obligations. Our email providers process that correspondence to deliver and store it. Support email is separate from encrypted vault storage: deleting your Hisar account does not automatically delete support messages. Never send your master password, Secret Key, Emergency Kit or vault contents.

Your rights and choices

We process hosted account data to provide the service under our contract with you. Website security logs and support handling rely on legitimate interests in operating a secure service and answering requests; applicable legal obligations may also require retention. The hosted-service policy gives the detailed legal bases.

Under applicable EU or UK data protection law, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email {{ support_email_link }} for data we control, or your own server's operator for a self-hosted account. We may verify your identity without asking for your master password or Secret Key.

You can complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, your local EU supervisory authority, or the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Hisar is not intended for children under 16.

Security limits and policy changes

Encryption does not protect an unlocked device from malware or erase data already shared with someone. Cloudflare's TLS termination does not itself decrypt vault contents, but the proxy and server are trusted with readable traffic and web pages they serve. A compromised share-link page could capture the link key and contents when opened. Verify recipients when sharing; organization vault access also depends on the server's team records.

We update the effective date when this policy changes and notify affected hosted-service users before significant changes take effect. The page's revision history is public. For questions or requests, see Contact and support or email {{ support_email_link }}.

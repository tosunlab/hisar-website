Questions

What's a founding member?

The first people who helped shape Hisar. Founding members have Pro free for life. It's a mark on your account, not a coupon: it never expires, needs no card, and shows as a badge in your account.

What happens if I stop paying?

You never lose access to what you have. Unlock, sync, SSH, hisar run, agents and export keep working. You just can't add more than Free allows until you remove some or renew. Nothing is deleted, and you can export everything at any time.

Why is yearly cheaper?

Every payment carries a fixed processing fee, which is a large share of {{ site.PRO_MONTHLY_PRICE }}. One payment a year keeps more of your money going into Hisar, so we pass the saving on.

Do you charge for agents, CI machines or approvals?

No per-agent or per-approval charges. Pro will include three service accounts for CI when they launch; agents on your Mac use hisar mcp for free.

Can I pay in the iPhone app?

No. Plans are managed on the web. The iPhone app shows your plan but doesn't sell anything.

Do prices include VAT? Who bills me?

Prices are in euros and include VAT for customers in the EU; VAT for your country is shown at checkout. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record: it handles the payment, sends your invoice and appears on your statement.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment, monthly or yearly. See the refund policy.
